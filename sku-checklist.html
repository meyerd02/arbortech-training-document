Catalogue Review · Per-SKU Checklist

Arbortech – SKU Review Checklist

A short run-through for one product at a time: pick a SKU, work down the list, and you'll have confirmed everything that matters — the SKU itself, visibility, collections, tags, filters, variants, imagery and price. Ten minutes per product, start to finish.

The checklist

1. Find the product & check its SKU 2. Confirm it's visible (three gates) 3. Check the tags 4. See it in its collections 5. Check the filter fields 6. Variants, voltage & price 7. Imagery 8. Final storefront pass

1. Find the product & check its SKU

Where: Shopify admin → Products → search by SKU or product name → open the product, SKU field is on each variant.

  • The SKU on each variant matches the master SKU list (searching the admin by the SKU finds the product directly).
  • If it's a bundle, kit or pack: no SKU on the bundle itself is correct — the component products carry the SKUs.
  • Title, description and variant names read correctly (voltage / size / pack quantity spelled out).

2. Confirm it's visible — the three gates

Where: all three are on the product page itself.

A product can look complete and still be invisible on the shop. All three gates must pass:

GateWhere to check
Status is ActiveStatus box, top of the product page
Published to Online StorePublishing / sales channels box
Included in the right marketsMarkets section of the publishing box

3. Check the tags

Where: Tags box, right-hand side of the product page.

Tags decide which collections the product joins — collections are rule-based, so the tags are the filing system. Every shoppable product should carry:

Tag groupValuesWhat it controls
Verticalpower-carving · masonry-trade · outdoor-grounds-careWhich vertical(s) list the product — a product can carry more than one
Product bucketpower-tools · attachments-accessories · spare-parts-consumablesWhich bucket it sits in, and which contextual filters its pages show
  • At least one vertical tag and the right bucket tag are present.
  • Spelling matches exactly — copy the tag from a similar existing product rather than typing it fresh.

4. See it in its collections

Where: on the storefront — the vertical's shop pages (e.g. Woodworking → Power Tools) — or admin → Products → Collections → open the collection and confirm the product is listed.

  • The product appears in every collection its tags say it should: its vertical(s), its bucket within each vertical, and Spare Parts & Consumables if it's a spare/consumable.
  • If the old site listed it under two verticals, it appears under both here.
  • Spare-parts-only items do not appear in accessories listings.

5. Check the filter fields

Where: metafields at the bottom of the product page (or ⋯ menu → Edit metafields). All three are pickers — you choose from a list, never type.

FieldFill it in when the product is…
Application typeA power tool or accessory
Compatible withAn accessory (which tools does it fit?)
Tool modelA spare part or consumable (which tool is it for?)

A blank field is not neutral

A product with an empty filter field disappears the moment a customer ticks any box in that filter — there is no "unassigned" group. If the value you need isn't in the picker list, ask us to add it; don't work around it.

6. Variants, voltage & price

Where: the variants list on the product page; prices per variant.

  • Every voltage / size / pack variant that should exist is there, and none that shouldn't.
  • Prices (and compare-at prices, if on promotion) are right per variant.
  • On the storefront, each market shows only its correct electrical options (e.g. a US shopper never sees 240V). If a variant is missing in one country only, tell us — that's the market routing rules, not the product.

7. Imagery

Where: the media gallery at the top of the product page.

  • At least one image, with the hero shot first — the first image is the tile shoppers see on collection pages.
  • Action/detail shots follow the hero, matching how the old site presented the product.

8. Final storefront pass

Where: the storefront, in a private/incognito window.

  • Search the product name — it comes up, and its page loads.
  • Open its collection page and find it via the sidebar filters (tick the value you set in step 5 — the product stays in the results).
  • Add it to the cart — the right variant, price and image carry through.

If a check fails

Re-check the three gates (step 2) and the tags (step 3) first — they explain most "missing product" cases. Anything that still doesn't add up, send us the SKU and which step it failed; that's everything we need to chase it.
