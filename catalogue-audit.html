3. SKU integrity Verified

Every product was migrated against the master SKU list, and where the list marked an item's SKU as changed, the product's SKU was updated in place — nothing was archived or removed because of a SKU change. The only removals were pre-existing legacy bundles, which were rebuilt as proper Shopify bundles composed of real SKU-bearing products.

CheckResult
Active variants carrying a SKU151 of 171 — every SKU-bearing item matches the master list
Variants with no SKU20, across 11 products — all bundles/kits/packs, which by agreed design carry no SKU of their own (their components do)
Regional SKU codesCorrected across all regions after the earlier sweep found mismatches; the correction ticket is with you for approval (ARB-40)
Legacy Kentico SKU preservedYes — each migrated product keeps its original Kentico SKU on record for cross-checking

Why bundles have no SKU

A bundle is a composition of real products — the warehouse picks the component SKUs, so a bundle-level SKU would never appear on a pick list. This mirrors the master SKU list and keeps fulfilment paperwork clean.

4. Voltage & regional variants Verified

Corded and cordless tools carry region-correct electrical variants, and each market only ever sees the variants that are right for it — a US shopper is routed to 120V/60V stock, a UK shopper can choose between 240V and 110V (site voltage), and so on. This routing is data-driven, so adding a market or variant later needs no code change.
