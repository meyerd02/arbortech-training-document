3. Cross-sell — Leigh-Ann's recommendation

How Leigh-Ann recommends an effective cross-sell works, as now reflected on the cart page:

  • On the cart page, before checkout — an "Add consumables and accessories to your order" strip sits between the cart items and the checkout button, catching the buyer at the moment of commitment without getting in the way of paying.
  • Matched to what's in the cart — with the Mini Grinder MG1000 in the cart, the suggestions are MG1000 cutting and diamond discs, not generic "you may also like" picks.
  • Consumables, not big-ticket items — low-consideration add-ons ($30–$95 against a ~$980 cart) that the buyer will genuinely need as blades and discs wear out.
  • One-click add — each suggestion goes straight into the cart from the same page; on desktop the order summary stays alongside, so the total updates in view.

Screenshots of both the desktop and mobile treatment are on file for the walkthrough.
